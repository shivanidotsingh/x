Epistemic Compass

This tool is about making sense of the world — noticing how you know what you know. It draws on the Theory of Knowledge (TOK) framework, which describes 8 ways of knowing: language, sense perception, emotion, reason, imagination, faith, intuition, and memory. Here, these are translated into two axes — vertical: Reason ↔ Faith; horizontal: Imagination ↔ Intuition.

Add a statement, see where it lands, and move it if it doesn’t feel right.
